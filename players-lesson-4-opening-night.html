Quick Write 24: Program Draft
Week 8 · 10 points · the Word file is graded
●
This is the whole Quick Write 24 assignment. Write the draft in Word, not in this lesson. The Word file is the graded file. Record the file name and word count below.
THE ASSIGNMENT

Write the first draft of your Director’s Essay in Word: 600 to 900 words of expository writing for a theatergoer. Include a thesis about how the scene builds meaning and mood, evidence from the play quoted and explained, a comparison of the text against a staged interpretation that names what each emphasizes and leaves out, and one layer your production will bring out. Use headings a reader can scan.

●
Focus question. How does Shakespeare build layers of meaning and mood in this scene, and which layer will your production bring out?
One thing to know before you start. This is a first draft. You revise it into the finished essay for your Director’s Program, so write the whole argument now and expect to improve it.

6
Build the program
Cover, essay, design page, cast and passage
●
Your Director’s Program has four parts. Build it in Word or PowerPoint, in this order, and export it to PDF.
1. COVER
The title, the scene by act and scene, and your name as director.
2. DIRECTOR’S ESSAY
700 to 1,000 words under headings a theatergoer can scan.
3. DESIGN PAGE
Setting and costume or color, each with a caption, a credit, and a one-line rationale.
4. CAST AND PASSAGE
A cast list with one-line character notes, and one cited passage.
IN WORD OR POWERPOINT
  • Build the four parts in order. Use real headings, not bold text, so a screen reader can find them.
  • Give every image a caption, a credit, and alt text that describes it for a reader who cannot see it.
  • Check that the essay runs 700 to 1,000 words. Cut padding if it runs long.
  • Export to PDF: in Word, File, Save As, PDF; in PowerPoint, File, Export, Create PDF.
  • Name the file LastName_FirstName_PlayersCompany_ELA10.pdf and save it in OneDrive: Optima → ELA → ELA10.
