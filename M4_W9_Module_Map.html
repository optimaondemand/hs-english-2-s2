Week 9
Week 9 Overview
The year closes with four rulers and four communities across ancient and modern literature, and with the craft of presenting a clear perspective aloud.
📖 READING
No new reading. Review the Odyssey, Oedipus the King, A Midsummer Night’s Dream, and The Great Gatsby.
📋 THIS WEEK’S LESSONS
Literature
1. Four Kings, Four Communities
Odysseus, Oedipus, Theseus with Oberon, and Gatsby compared across periods; what a ruler and a self-governing person owe a community.
Literature
2. Presenting with a Clear Perspective
Thesis, logical order, credible evidence, and delivery: eye contact, rate, pause, volume, enunciation, gesture.
🔎 WHAT TO TRACK WHILE READING
▶
The source of each ruler’s authority: crown, command, will, or fortune.
▶
What each community needs, and what it does when its ruler fails.
▶
How period and culture change each work’s answer to the same question.
